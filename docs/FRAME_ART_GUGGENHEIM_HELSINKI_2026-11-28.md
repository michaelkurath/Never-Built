# Guggenheim Helsinki on TRMNL Frame

Verified 28 November 2026.

The full Guggenheim Helsinki proposal was not built. Helsinki City Council decision §306, formally dated 30 November 2016, rejects implementation; the final vote recorded 32 in favour and 53 against. The architects identify Art in the City as their winning 2015 competition entry and describe ten fragmented volumes with charred-wood facades and a timber structure. Their project gallery preserves drawings, rendered views and a photograph of physical architectural study models (MK_GUG_MDL_ALL_HD.jpg). These physical maquettes are design studies, not an operational museum or a full-scale building prototype. No partial construction of this proposal is established by these records. Existing harbour fabric and later South Harbour projects are separate. Our two illustrations are imagined presentation maquettes, not replicas or photographs of a specific historical model or completed museum. They interpret the low dark pavilion cluster and taller glazed-crown tower; pavilion counts, arrangement, roof forms, tower proportions, glazing, paths, vegetation, people, materials, model base and harbour geometry are simplified or interpretive rather than exact technical reconstructions. Initial portrait drafts with tight framing were rejected. Reference images were not supplied to the generator or embedded in the exports.
