## Complete generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: NEVER BUILT museum exhibit artistic reconstruction, responsive 2:1 master with safe 4:3 center crop. Primary request: Create an original monochrome aerial relief-model interpretation of Frank Lloyd Wright's unbuilt Broadacre City concept. Scene/backdrop: a vast horizontal planned landscape presented like a carefully crafted architectural model on a neutral white museum ground. Subject: dispersed one-acre homesteads and small farm plots, orchards and fields, a branching highway system with an overpass, small civic and commercial clusters, a few slender isolated towers used as community landmarks, light industry and an airfield integrated across the landscape. It must read as one coherent decentralised master plan, not a real completed city. Style/medium: black-ink architectural engraving combined with relief-model texture, restrained crosshatching, strong high-level pattern, e-ink friendly, no pre-dithering. Composition/framing: high oblique aerial view, landscape 2:1; the complete model footprint centered in the middle two-thirds so a 4:3 crop preserves farms, highway and at least one civic tower; clean white margins. Lighting/mood: neutral museum-documentation lighting, visionary but analytical. Constraints: explicitly an artistic interpretation of a paper/model proposal; drawings and the 12-foot model were presentation material, not construction. Avoid: dense downtown skyline, modern megacity, suburban photorealism, identifiable existing Wright buildings, readable labels, logos, people in foreground, color, borders, watermark.
