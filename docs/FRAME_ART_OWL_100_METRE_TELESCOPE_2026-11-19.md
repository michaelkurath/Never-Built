# OWL 100-Metre Telescope on TRMNL Frame

Verified 19 November 2026.

ESO completed the 100-metre OWL concept study in 2005. A review recommended a smaller telescope because of technical and schedule risks. The full OWL telescope was never constructed. The OWL Blue Book records optical material and mirror-blank demonstrators, including a small CESIC blank, and contemporary research describes silicon-carbide segment demonstrators. These were component tests. The later 39-metre ELT is a separate telescope design. This artwork interprets the 2004 open steel configuration. The terrain, truss spacing and number of drawn mirror segments are illustrative. No constructed OWL site is depicted.
