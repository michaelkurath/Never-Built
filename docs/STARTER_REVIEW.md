# Source review — 7 October 2026

## Live

### The Illinois

[Frank Lloyd Wright Foundation](https://franklloydwright.org/frank-lloyd-wrights-unbuilt-skyscrapers-come-to-life-with-never-before-seen-3d-imagery/)
documents the 1956 announcement, mile-high/528-storey proposal, and absence
of a site, client or budget. It was not a funded construction project.
Generated artwork is interpretive; its skyline includes later buildings.

### Ford Nucleon

[The Henry Ford brochure](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/403679)
and [model record](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/278186)
identify 1958, a non-working 3/8-scale model, and assumptions about small,
safe nuclear reactors. The museum calls it more public relations than serious
development. There was no running nuclear car. Artwork interprets the styling.

### Project Orion

[NASA overview](https://pwg.gsfc.nasa.gov/stargaze/Snucfly.htm),
[December 1964 memo](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v34/d28),
and [Smithsonian test vehicle](https://airandspace.si.edu/collection-objects/propulsion-test-vehicle-project-orion/nasm_A19721008000)
support the 1958 beginning, conventional-explosive model tests, nuclear-test
and fallout obstacles, and withdrawal of support. The memo records support
ending 31 December 1964; Smithsonian dates cancellation to 1965. Preserve
the starred research period. The nuclear spacecraft stayed unbuilt, while
test hardware existed. Artwork is conceptual, not one exact final blueprint.

## Candidates

### Boeing 2707 — 24/25

[Boeing product history](https://www.boeing.com/content/dam/boeing/boeingdotcom/history/pdf/Boeing_Products.pdf)
and [company chronology](https://www.boeing.com/content/dam/boeing/v2/company/history/pdf/Boeing-Chronology.pdf)
were retrieved through indexed source excerpts: 1966 selection, 1971 funding
cancellation before prototype completion. Full PDFs could not be fetched;
fact review remains pending. Identify early swing-wing versus later fixed-wing
design, and verify surviving mock-up sections before promotion.

### Tatlin's Tower — 21/25

[Centre Pompidou](https://www.centrepompidou.fr/en/ressources/oeuvre/cqGrB5n)
confirms the unbuilt structure, surviving drawings/models/photos, intertwined
spirals and glass volumes, and its 1979 reconstruction. It does not establish
a sufficiently specific construction-failure reason. Research that before
promotion; do not present the reconstruction as Tatlin's original model.

### Project Daedalus — 22/25

[British Interplanetary Society](https://www.bis-space.com/technical-projects/)
documents the 1973–1978 volunteer study, 1978 report, two-stage fusion concept
and proposed Barnard's Star flyby. The study was completed, not cancelled.
Speculative engineering assumptions were not demonstrated by a flight.

All candidates remain pending full verification, text, image and final review.
Scores are editorial judgements, not historical measurements.
