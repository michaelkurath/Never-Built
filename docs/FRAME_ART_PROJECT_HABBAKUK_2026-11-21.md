# Project Habbakuk on TRMNL Frame

Verified 21 November 2026.

The full ocean-going pykrete aircraft carrier was never built. NRC Canada distinguishes its proposed massive floating airstrip from the secret Alberta prototype. The Alberta Aviation Museum documents the approximately 60 by 30 foot floating test model at Patricia Lake, built in 1943 to test the concept and refrigeration. The test model was abandoned and subsequently melted and sank; piping and asphalt remnants remain on the lake bed. It was experimental hardware, not partial construction of the full operational carrier. The artwork interprets the proposed full-size carrier with a flat deck, massive slab hull and period aircraft for scale. Hull proportions, superstructure, deck fittings, aircraft arrangement and seascape are artistic interpretations rather than a definitive engineering drawing. These are not pictures of the lake prototype.
