## Proposed exhibit 045: Pittsburgh Point Park Civic Center

[Avery’s 1947 scheme 1](https://www.jstor.org/stable/community.36561659) is explicitly unbuilt. The [Guggenheim exhibition record](https://www.guggenheim.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/gen-press-frank-lloyd-wright-outward-within-kit.pdf) identifies the scale model as fabricated in 2009, not a prototype or completed building. [Point State Park](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dcnr/recreation/where-to-go/state-parks/find-a-park/point-state-park) is a separate completed landscape. The [NPS nomination](https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/9bd54cee-5aeb-4d8e-982f-6ad287c99628) describes the spiral roads and circular complex.
