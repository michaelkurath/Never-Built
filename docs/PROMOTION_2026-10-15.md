# Collection review — 15 October 2026

The Palace of the Soviets is exhibit 015 (24/25). The Design Museum identifies Boris Iofan’s winning design among Moscow’s unrealised architectural landmarks and records construction beginning in 1937, termination after the 1941 German invasion, reuse of the steel frame and conversion of the foundations into a pool. The entry and artwork explicitly distinguish drawings, models, foundations and partial steelwork from a completed palace.

The Chicago Spire is the new provisional candidate (23/25: recognition 4, visual strength 5, story strength 5, evidence clarity 5, catalogue fit 4). Specialist tall-building and replacement-project records document that construction began and left a deep foundation/cofferdam, while no habitable tower was completed. The currently built 400 Lake Shore Drive towers are a different SOM design, not a completion of the Spire.
