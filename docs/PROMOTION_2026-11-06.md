# Collection review — 6 November 2026

## Comet Rendezvous / Asteroid Flyby — exhibit 035, 21/25

Verified 6 November 2026. JPL announced CRAF’s termination in February 1992. GAO records preliminary design and cancellation of the shared Mariner Mark II design. Design records and component development do not constitute a completed CRAF flight spacecraft. Cassini flew separately on a redesigned spacecraft. The artwork is an interpretive model based on GAO figure 1.1, not a documented physical artifact.

- [NASA JPL — 1992 termination announcement](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/budget-reductions-to-two-planetary-exploration-projects/)
- [NASA NTRS — CRAF mission study](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930010071): Records congressional approval in 1989 and the planned Mariner Mark II design.
- [US GAO — CRAF and Cassini review](https://www.gao.gov/assets/nsiad-94-24.pdf): Describes early cancellation and the later Cassini-specific redesign.

## New candidate: Washington Monument Colonnade — 23/25

Checked 6 November 2026. The candidate is Robert Mills’s unbuilt colonnaded rotunda only, not the Washington Monument as a whole. The obelisk was completed in a revised form in 1884. The Library of Congress print represents the proposal, not evidence of construction.

- [National Park Service — construction timeline](https://home.nps.gov/wamo/learn/historyculture/monumentconstruction.htm)
- [Library of Congress — original design print](https://www.loc.gov/item/2003656330/): Shows Mills’s original proposed colonnade and explains the distinction from the completed monument.
- [NPS — The Washington Monument in Its Setting](https://www.nps.gov/parkhistory/online_books/wamo/stones.pdf): Explicitly states the grand colonnade was never built.
