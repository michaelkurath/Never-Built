# Collection review — 4 November 2026

## Promoted: Tetrahedron City

Tetrahedron City becomes exhibit 033 at 23/25. MoMA explicitly describes Fuller and Sadao’s c. 1968 Yomiuriland proposal as never built: a two-mile-edge floating tetrahedron intended for one million residents and 300,000 apartments around an interior harbor.

- Primary source: [MoMA — Tetrahedron City Project](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/863)
- Fit: an exceptionally legible megastructure with direct museum evidence.
- Qualification: MoMA’s aerial perspective and any related drawings or models are presentation records, not a completed city, inhabited prototype or constructed floating megastructure.
- Artwork: an original neutral concept-model interpretation; it does not copy the museum image or claim built reality.

## New candidate: Contemporary City for Three Million

Contemporary City for Three Million enters the queue at 22/25: recognition 4, visual strength 4, story strength 4, evidence clarity 5, catalogue fit 5.

Fondation Le Corbusier labels the 1922 project “Not located” and “Projet non réalisé,” and includes it among unrealized projects. The 27-metre Salon d’Automne stand and 100-square-metre diorama were exhibition material, not a city, inhabited district or full-scale prototype. The proposal is distinct from the later Plan Voisin already in the live catalogue.

- [Project record](https://www.fondationlecorbusier.fr/en/work-architecture/projects-contemporary-city-of-three-million-inhabitants-not-located-1922/)
- [Unrealized-project classification](https://www.fondationlecorbusier.fr/en/le-corbusier/works/architecture/unrealized-projects/)
