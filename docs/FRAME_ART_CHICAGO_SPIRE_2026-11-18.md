# The Chicago Spire on TRMNL Frame

Verified 18 November 2026.

The Chicago Planning Commission approved Calatrava’s tapering, twisting 150-storey scheme in 2007. The design was presented in renderings and a scale model. The project reached foundation work, leaving a deep circular cofferdam. Construction stalled during the financial crisis and the tower never rose. A separate project, 400 Lake Shore, subsequently used the site. The artwork interprets how the 2007 proposal might have appeared from the waterfront.
