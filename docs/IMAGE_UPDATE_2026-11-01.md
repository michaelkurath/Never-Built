# The Peak artwork — 1 November 2026

## Scope

Original architectural-model interpretation for exhibit 030. The image conveys stacked, displaced geological plates and long cantilevers on steep terrain. It is deliberately model-like and does not portray a completed club. Its geometry and viewpoint do not copy a Zaha Hadid Foundation painting, drawing, model photograph or MoMA work.

## Generation prompt

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset: NEVER BUILT e-paper museum exhibit. Create an original monochrome architectural-model interpretation of an unbuilt early-1980s avant-garde private leisure club proposed for a steep Hong Kong hillside, inspired by the abstract idea of stacked geological plates but not copying any Zaha Hadid painting, drawing, model photograph or composition. On a plain white museum-table background, show a complete physical presentation model: several long, thin, sharply angled cantilevered slabs and shard-like horizontal volumes stacked around a central void, embedded into an abstract steep rocky terrain base. Make it visibly a proposal model, not a completed inhabited building: clean model materials, small neutral scale blocks, no photoreal city, no construction site. Original viewpoint and geometry. Black ink engraving, restrained crosshatching, broad grayscale separation, strong silhouette, no pre-dithering. Landscape 2:1. Keep the entire model, terrain base, longest cantilevers and shadow fully inside the centered 4:3 safe zone with at least 15% breathing room; outer wings blank. No readable text, labels, logos, people, copied artwork, border or watermark.
