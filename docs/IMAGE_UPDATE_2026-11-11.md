## Complete prompt

Use case: historical-scene. Original monochrome engraving of a small architectural concept maquette of Christian Jank’s 1883 ideal design for Ludwig II’s unbuilt Falkenstein castle. Reference image is the documented 1883 gouache, for design motifs only, not a composition to copy. Use a fresh three-quarter view with a low plain maquette base, a compact jagged rock pinnacle, ornate Gothic palace mass with steep clustered gables, high slender round tower with a broad corbelled crown and pointed spire, lower crenellated retaining walls and a small pointed gatehouse. Complete peak, tower and rock base visible; isolated on white paper with generous margin. No landscape, medieval ruin, Neuschwanstein, people, text, labels, border, logos or watermark. Crisp bold silhouettes and restrained fine crosshatching, strictly grayscale, museum e-ink editorial illustration, unmistakably an imagined model, not an archival photo or an exact reconstruction.

Design reference: [Bavarian Palace Administration’s publication of Jank’s 1883 ideal design](https://schloesserblog.bayern.de/geheimnisse/falkenstein-ein-projekt-ludwigs-ii). The reference is not redistributed as an exhibit asset.
