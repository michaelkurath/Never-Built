# Newton’s Cenotaph on TRMNL Frame

Verified 17 November 2026.

Étienne-Louis Boullée’s 1784 proposal for Isaac Newton’s cenotaph remained a paper project. The BnF preserves the drawings, and The Menil Collection reproduces the geometric elevation in its exhibition guide. Later interpretations include Bernd Grimm’s alabaster-plaster scale model. The sources document no full-size prototype or partial construction.

- [Bibliothèque nationale de France, original 1784 section](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb45191199m)
