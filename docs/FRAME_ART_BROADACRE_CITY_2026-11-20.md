# Broadacre City on TRMNL Frame

Verified 20 November 2026.

Frank Lloyd Wright’s complete Broadacre City master plan was never built. Columbia’s archive explicitly catalogues the master plan as an unbuilt project. The 1934–1935 presentation model, made from painted wood, cardboard and paper, survives at MoMA. Wright’s foundation also documents ten collateral models shown with the large model in 1935. The physical models were presentation material. The cited records identify no full-scale Broadacre prototype or partial construction of the full scheme. Later buildings influenced by Wright are distinct from implementation of the master plan. This artwork is an original model-like interpretation of dispersed homesteads, fields, roads and civic elements, with an invented arrangement and terrain.
