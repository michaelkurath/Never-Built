## Complete prompt

Original black-ink museum catalogue engraving for monochrome e-paper: interpret Frank Lloyd Wright’s unbuilt 1940 Crystal Heights (Crystal City) as a small architectural presentation model. Cluster numerous slender pale rectilinear high-rise towers, edged in closely repeated projecting horizontal floor plates, articulated by a strong vertical core, staggered at different elevations on a broad low sloping terraced podium. Emphasize thin cantilevered balconies, broad horizontal terraces and interlocking groups of towers, not generic smooth glass boxes. Slightly elevated three-quarter view, entire low model plinth visible with ample white margins. Crisp contours, limited hatching, pure white background, no cast shadows, cars, model labels, lettering, skyline or existing Washington Hilton. Interpretive architectural model, not a photograph, measured drawing or reproduction of an archival illustration.
