## Sources, fit and ratings

MoMA identifies Ron Herron's 1964 Cities: Moving aerial perspective and describes it as architectural commentary rather than a serious structural proposition: https://www.moma.org/collection/works/813 . V&A explicitly lists Walking City among experimental projects never built: https://www.vam.ac.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0003/178113/58425_file.pdf (page 23). Centre Pompidou corroborates its 1964 architectural-drawing status: https://www.centrepompidou.fr/en/ressources/oeuvre/c5z5bG . This authored conceptual architecture is in scope, but is not presented as engineered, contracted or cancelled construction. Museum drawings/display models do not demonstrate a functioning full-size pod. The separate 1966 ocean drawing is not the selected version.

Guggenheim Helsinki: official council minutes §306 of 30 November 2016 reject implementation (53–32): https://www.hel.fi/static/public/hela/Kaupunginvaltuusto/Suomi/Paatoshistoria/2016/Kanslia_2016-11-30_Kvsto_21_Pk/2F6CB8C1-485D-C014-86D7-58DD4A200002/Guggenheim_Helsinki_-taidemuseohankkeen_toteuttami.html . Moreau Kusunoki documents the 2015 winning entry: https://moreaukusunoki.com/project/guggenheim-helsinki/ . Competition models/images are not a completed museum. The period is scoped to the selected design and decision, not the earlier feasibility process. Rating: recognition 4, visual 4, story 4, evidence clarity 5, fit 5 = 22/25.
