# HL-20 on TRMNL Frame

Verified 27 November 2026.

NASA explicitly states that no HL-20 flight hardware was built during the 1989–1993 studies. Real research hardware did exist: scale aerodynamic models were tested in wind tunnels, and a full-scale non-flying engineering mockup was built in 1990 by North Carolina State University and North Carolina A&T with NASA guidance for systems and human-factors evaluation. NASA-CR-187801 documents its fabrication. The complete proposed operational flight system was not built; the mockup and wind-tunnel models are not flying spacecraft or partial construction of an operational fleet. HL-42 and later Dream Chaser are separate successor designs, not completed HL-20 vehicles. These two illustrations are imagined small display maquettes interpreting the early rounded lifting-body configuration, with two outward-canted rear tip fins and a smaller central rear fin. NASA’s overview photograph of a wind-tunnel model informed the visual shape; it is not embedded, traced or supplied to the generator. The stand, model materials, colours, cockpit, proportions and details are interpretive, not an exact replica or photograph of the 1990 full-scale artifact. No present storage location or display availability of that artifact is asserted.
