Today’s 400 Lake Shore Drive towers are a separate SOM design and are not described as completion of the Spire.

Atlantropa is the new provisional candidate (24/25: recognition 4, visual strength 5, story strength 5, evidence clarity 5, catalogue fit 5). The candidate explicitly treats archival objects and later visualisation as design evidence, not a full-scale model, operational prototype, completed dam or altered sea.
