## Status and sources

The Design Museum identifies Boris Iofan's winning design among Moscow's unbuilt landmarks. Construction began in 1937 and stopped after the 1941 invasion; foundation work and lower steelwork existed, but the complete palace and Lenin-topped tower were never built. The steel was repurposed, and the site later became a pool before the cathedral was rebuilt.
