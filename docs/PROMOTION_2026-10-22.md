## Source and fit review

MoMA preserves the 1934–1935 Broadacre City model and identifies the work as a proposed development concept; its gallery label says the farm units were never built and records Wright’s unsuccessful effort to obtain a commission: https://www.moma.org/collection/works/161773 . The archive inventory separately classifies the master plan, model and component studies as unbuilt. The large model and drawings are presentation material, not a completed city or operational prototype; later influence is not partial completion.

New candidate Plan Voisin: Fondation Le Corbusier documents the 1925 Paris project and its drawings, while MoMA explicitly states that the plan was never built and describes its polemical urban purpose: https://www.fondationlecorbusier.fr/oeuvre-architecture/projets-plan-voisin-paris-france-1925/ and https://www.moma.org/audio/playlist/269/3495 . Drawings, publications and a diorama are presentation material, not demolition works, completed towers or a prototype district. Rating: recognition 4, visual strength 5, story strength 5, evidence clarity 5, catalogue fit 5 = 24/25.
