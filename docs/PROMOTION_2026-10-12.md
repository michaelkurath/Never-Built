# Collection review — 12 October 2026

Manned Orbiting Laboratory is exhibit 012 (23/25). NASA records cancellation on 10 June 1969 and distinguishes the proposed 60-foot crewed stations from the 1966 orbital test, which carried a payload-less MOL mockup and refurbished Gemini-B capsule. Aside from long-lead imaging mirrors, NASA says no operational flight hardware existed at cancellation. The artwork is therefore labelled as a reconstruction of the unbuilt operational station.

Sea Dragon is the new provisional candidate (22/25: recognition 3, visual strength 5, story strength 5, evidence clarity 4, catalogue fit 5). NASA archives the enormous sea-launched rocket as a feasibility and cost study. Smithsonian preserves Robert Truax's project papers and documents the absence of institutional support for related later concepts. The entry explicitly distinguishes small related sea-launch tests from a full Sea Dragon vehicle or operational prototype, neither of which was built.
