# Tokyo Bay Plan 1960 on TRMNL Frame

Verified 26 November 2026.

Kenzo Tange’s full A Plan for Tokyo 1960 was never realized. Harvard’s Frances Loeb Library case study explicitly identifies it as unbuilt and preserves drawings, perspectives and a plan view of a physical presentation model. Tange Associates records the named 1960 master plan and reference images. These are design and model records, not evidence of completed transport infrastructure. No full-scale operational prototype or partial construction of this particular plan is established by these sources; later Tokyo Bay bridges, reclamation and districts are separate works, not completion of this proposal. These two images are imagined architectural maquettes interpreting the civic spine, looping transport system and branching A-frame housing. They are not photographs or replicas of an archival model. Materials, colours, module counts, loop geometry, scale, base and viewpoints are interpretive; no disputed total-length claim is repeated.
