# Live and candidate process

`data/trmnl.json` is live only. `data/candidates.json` is the research queue;
it is excluded from polling and the website.

Candidates retain GOODBYE's four review gates: fact_check, text_review,
image_review and final_approval. Initial ratings are provisional. Promotion
requires verified facts and scope, concise text, original responsive images,
device previews and final review. Routine promotion within an authorised task
does not require another user confirmation.

Score recognition, visual strength, story strength, evidence clarity and
catalogue fit from 1–5 each, total out of 25. Evidence clarity replaces
GOODBYE's endpoint clarity: a proposal needs clear documented status but not
necessarily a single cancellation date.

Do not invent why a project remained unbuilt. Distinguish proposals, models,
partial construction, experimental hardware and working prototypes. Fictional
designs are outside scope. Completed feasibility studies are not cancelled
construction programmes.

To promote: remove the candidate, assign a unique live exhibit number, add
reviewed text and image metadata, run all checks, rebuild the website, inspect
device previews, and commit. Counts are derived from the live data.
