# Fun Palace and Project Daedalus promotion — 7 October 2026

## Source and text review

Fun Palace, exhibit 005, reviewed score 22/25 (2, 5, 5, 5, 5).
[CCA's project history](https://www.cca.qc.ca/en/articles/91951/dangerous-immoral-ahead-of-its-time)
confirms the 1961–1974 project period, restrictive policies, community concerns
and failure to agree on a site. [MoMA](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/846)
confirms it was intended for actual construction. Its 1959–1961 storyboard dates
are distinguished from the broader project period.
[CCA's 1964 brochure](https://www.cca.qc.ca/en/articles/issues/2/what-the-future-looked-like/32737/1964-fun-palace)
and archive document the surviving records and adaptable structure. Artwork
interprets that brochure's gantry, framework, modules and bridges, with no
claim to recreate a specific approved site. Today's community events are separate.

Project Daedalus, exhibit 006, reviewed score 22/25 (3, 5, 5, 5, 4).
[BIS's Technical Projects history](https://www.bis-space.com/technical-projects/)
confirms the 1973–1978 thirteen-member volunteer study, 1978 report, uncrewed
two-stage fusion concept and proposed Barnard's Star flyby. The design study
was completed, not cancelled; no funded spacecraft construction programme is
inferred. Artwork uses the staged bell/tank/truss configuration in BIS-hosted
concept imagery, with interpretative details and no claimed flight demonstration.

All display strings meet layout limits. Both exhibits retain the explicit
artistic-reconstruction metadata used by the website and alt text.
