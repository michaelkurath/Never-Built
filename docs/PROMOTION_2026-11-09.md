## Promotion: Superconducting Super Collider, exhibit 038

Reviewed 21/25: recognition 4, visual strength 3, story 5, evidence clarity 5, catalogue fit 4. [DOE OIG audit](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2015/09/f26/DOE-OIG-0389.pdf) documents the proposed 54-mile racetrack ring, escalating costs, management issues and 28 October 1993 termination. [DOE’s 1995 environmental decision](https://www.energy.gov/index.php/nepa/articles/ea-1090-finding-no-significant-impact) explicitly refers to the partially constructed linac; it proposes medical reuse but does not prove that reuse succeeded. [Smithsonian magnet-component record](https://www.si.edu/object/sections-magnets-superconducting-super-collider:nmah_1452617) supports real partial hardware and ring identity. Scope is the never-completed SSC, not a claim nothing was built. Display title SUPER COLLIDER is shortened for device readability. *1983 is the advisory-panel recommendation, not groundbreaking.

## New sourced candidate

Analytical Engine — provisional 24/25: 4 + 5 + 5 + 5 + 5. [Science Museum Group’s Babbage account](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/people/ap8) explicitly says the machine was never completed and describes funding limitations and continuing revisions. [Its trial-model record](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co62245/babbages-analytical-engine-1834-1871-trial-model) documents a real portion of the mill and printing mechanism. Scope is the complete programmable engine. The real trial pieces and the museum’s working 1991 Difference Engine No.2 are not completion of that engine. 1834–1871 is conception through Babbage’s death/trial-model period, not one frozen final design.
