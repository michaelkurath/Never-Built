# Frame artwork for four additional exhibits

Each image is an original generated illustration. The two orientations use separate compositions. The RGB JPEGs are exported at 2560 × 1440 in landscape and 1440 × 2560 in portrait. Keep the upper-right corner clear for the caption card. The vehicle and building details are interpretive.

- **Fun Palace** shows the open steel framework, overhead gantry and suspended platforms. [Canadian Centre for Architecture](https://www.cca.qc.ca/en/articles/91951/dangerous-immoral-ahead-of-its-time)
- **Ford Nucleon** uses the red and black palette recorded for the nonfunctional scale model. [The Henry Ford](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/278186)
- **Sea Dragon** shows an imagined ocean deployment of the proposed vehicle at rest. [NASA Technical Reports Server](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19880069339)
- **Tatlin’s Tower** uses rust-red framework against pale paper to separate the spirals and suspended volumes. [Centre Pompidou](https://www.centrepompidou.fr/en/ressources/oeuvre/cqGrB5n)
